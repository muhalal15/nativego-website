About NativeGo

It connects tourists visiting Kenya with verified local guides who live in the places they show you.

The idea is simple. A first-time visitor to Nairobi, Lamu or the Mara usually depends on hotel desks, random touts or guesswork. A local guide removes that guesswork: they know the safe routes, the fair prices, the languages and the stories behind the places.

Our mission

To make travel in Kenya safer, fairer and more personal — for visitors and for guides. Visitors get honest local knowledge. Guides, many of whom work informally today, get visible profiles, public reviews and a fair way to be found and paid.

How guides are verified

No one appears on NativeGo without passing these checks:

1. Identity confirmed

Each guide submits a national ID and a phone number that is confirmed before the profile goes live.

2. Experience reviewed

We check where the guide has worked, which languages they speak, and what kind of trips they have led before.

3. Reviews kept public

After every trip, travellers leave a rating that stays visible on the guide's profile. Guides cannot hide or delete reviews.

Why trust matters

Trust is the whole product. A tourist hands a stranger their day, their money and sometimes their safety. That is why verification comes before listing, why prices are shown up front in Kenya shillings, and why payment goes through M-Pesa with a record both sides can see. If a trip goes wrong, the review system and the helpline are the traveller's backup.
