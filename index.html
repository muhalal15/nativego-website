See Kenya through local eyes

NativeGo connects tourists with verified local guides across Kenya. Instead of guessing your way around a new town, you travel with someone who lives there, speaks the local languages, and knows which matatu stage, market stall and viewpoint are worth your time.

Every guide on this site is checked before they are listed: identity confirmed, experience reviewed, and past travellers' ratings kept public. You book the person, not just the place.

Request a Guide

How it works in 3 steps

1. Tell us your trip

Fill in the request form with your destination, travel date and group size. It takes about two minutes.

2. Meet your matched guide

We match you with a verified guide for your destination and share their profile, languages and price before you commit.

3. Explore, then rate

Travel together, pay through M-Pesa, and leave a public review so the next traveller can book with confidence.
