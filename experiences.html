Experiences on offer

Each experience is led by a verified local guide. Prices are per person in Kenya shillings and include the guide; park fees, transport and meals are confirmed with the guide before you pay.

When to go

Dry months (January to March, July to October) are best for safaris and hikes. The coast is warm all year, but carry rain gear from November to December.

Weekend slots fill fast — request at least three days ahead.
