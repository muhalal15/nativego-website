The booking journey

Booking a guide on NativeGo takes five steps, and you are never locked in before you have seen your guide's profile and price.

  1. Send a request. Fill in the request form with your destination, travel date and group size.
  2. Get matched. We match you with a verified guide for your destination and send you their profile, languages and price.
  3. Confirm and pay. Once you agree, you confirm and pay through M-Pesa. You get a payment record you can keep.
  4. Meet and explore. Meet your guide at the agreed place and time, and enjoy the trip.
  5. Rate your guide. After the trip, leave a public review so the next traveller can book with confidence.

Safety and trust

Verified guides only

Identity confirmed and experience reviewed before any guide is listed. Unverified guides never appear on the site.

Public reviews

Every rating stays visible on the guide's profile. Guides cannot hide or delete reviews, so a high rating actually means something.

Payment with a record

Payment goes through M-Pesa, so both sides keep a record of what was paid. Agree the full price with your guide before the trip, and never pay extra cash to strangers along the way.
